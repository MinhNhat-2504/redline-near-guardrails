## “What did you build?” draft

REDLINE lets a person give an AI executor a spending budget without handing over an unrestricted wallet. The owner reviews and signs a policy defining recipients, spending and transaction limits, expiry and cooldown. A native NEAR vault enforces these rules, reserves funds before asynchronous dispatch, and finalizes accounting through private callbacks. A FunctionCall key restricts the executor to the vault's transfer method; the owner can revoke new spending and withdraw available principal.

The Testnet prototype includes wallet sign-in, reviewed funding/permissions/withdrawal, durable request recovery, a deterministic policy checker, receipt history and a bilingual guided evidence demo. Public transaction proofs cover an allowed payment and cap, recipient, replay and revoked rejections. Optional consented Gemini explanations cannot sign or override the contract; provider reliability remains limited. Payment requests are controlled test cases, not autonomous service purchases. The repo preserves the original Solana baseline and documents the new NEAR work, reproduction, tests and license scope. Mainnet and professional audit are out of scope.
